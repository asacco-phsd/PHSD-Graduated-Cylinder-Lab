🧪 The Graduated Cylinder Virtual Lab

How to use the labDrag the pitcher onto the test tube, cup, or beaker to fill it. Drag a filled test tube, cup, or beaker onto the graduated cylinder to pour it in. Drag any filled piece of equipment onto the sink to empty it. The cylinder only holds 100 mL — watch what happens if you try to pour in more. Use your handout to record what you measure.
